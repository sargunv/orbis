Orbis is yours for this session. Survey it first: the code, the open issues,
the live demo, and where the map-rendering landscape is now (MapLibre and
its Rust port mln-rs, research, new GPU and web features). Orbis aims to
render the MapLibre v8 style spec, plus projections and coordinate reference
systems, better than mln-rs: smaller, faster to load, faster at processing
tiles and faster at drawing them, in 2D and 3D (AGENTS.md). Decide what
would move it furthest toward that, then build it. That might be cleanup,
refactoring, deletion, performance, bug fixes, spec coverage, demos or
documentation, in whatever mix you choose. Be ambitious, stay inside the
scope, and leave the codebase simpler than you found it. Triage the issues
as you go. If the owner gives a budget or a focus, work within it. When you
finish, report what changed, with numbers (sizes, timings, fixture counts)
and screenshots.
