name: wave
description: Take the helm of Orbis and improve it however you judge best. Use when asked to run a wave, work on whatever you want, or improve the project.
---

Orbis is yours for this session. Survey it first: the code, the open issues,
the live demo, and where the map-rendering landscape is now (MapLibre,
deck.gl, research, new GPU and web features). Decide what would make Orbis
most impressive and most solid, then build it. That might be cleanup,
refactoring, deletion, performance, bug fixes, new features, demos or
documentation, in whatever mix you choose. Be ambitious: pick work that shows
what the engine can do, and leave the codebase simpler than you found it.
Triage the issues as you go. If the owner gives a budget or a focus, work
within it. When you finish, report what changed, with numbers and
screenshots.
