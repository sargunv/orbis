# Third-party code and data

Orbis's own code is under the BSD-3-Clause licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
This file lists what the tree ports, vendors or mirrors from other projects,
and the terms each carries. Rust and npm dependencies are fetched by Cargo and
pnpm and keep their own licences; they are not repeated here.

## Code ported into Orbis

| What | Where | From | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Line extrusion and line atlas, fill-extrusion layout, symbol layout and placement (`symbol_layout.ts`, `quads.ts`, `get_anchors.ts`, `clip_line.ts`, `merge_lines.ts`, `collision_feature.ts`, `collision_index.ts`, `grid_index.ts`, `cross_tile_symbol_index.ts`, `placement.ts`, `projection.ts`, `find_pole_of_inaccessibility.ts`, ...), text shaping, line breaking, bidi line rules and text transforms (`shaping.ts`, `bidi.ts`, `transform_text.ts`, `arabic_shaping.ts`, `graphemes.ts`), glyph PBF parsing, overzoom, and the WGSL shaders that follow GL JS's GLSL (circle, heatmap, line, fill, symbol, sky, raster, hillshade, color-relief, extrusion, DEM preparation) | `orb-layout`, `orb-render`, `orb-symbol`, `orb-text`, `orb-tile`, `orb-shaders`, `xtask/src/unicode.rs` | [MapLibre GL JS](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js) at `2b68611a4e2d4f7f076f6c6ebf758e2d53f91471` | BSD-3-Clause; [`crates/orb-layout/src/LICENSE-MapLibre`](crates/orb-layout/src/LICENSE-MapLibre) |
| Tile cover: the default level of detail by distance and pitch (`covering_tiles.ts`), and on the globe the fixed zoom up to 4, the tile volumes' planes through the planet's centre and the frustum cut off at the horizon (`globe_covering_tiles_details_provider.ts`, `frustum.ts`) | `crates/orb-camera/src/lib.rs` | MapLibre GL JS at the commit above | BSD-3-Clause; as above |
| Expression parsing and type checking (`ParsingContext`), evaluation semantics, Lab/HCL colour spaces (which include a portion of d3-color, BSD-3-Clause, Mike Bostock) | `orb-expr` | [MapLibre style spec](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-style-spec) 26.5.1 | BSD-3-Clause; [`crates/orb-expr/tests/data/LICENSE-style-spec.txt`](crates/orb-expr/tests/data/LICENSE-style-spec.txt) |
| GeoJSON tiling | `crates/orb-tile/src/geojson` | [`@maplibre/geojson-vt`](https://github.com/maplibre/geojson-vt) 6.3.0 | MIT and ISC; [`crates/orb-tile/src/geojson/LICENSE`](crates/orb-tile/src/geojson/LICENSE) |

| Cubic Bézier easing | `crates/orb-expr/src/eval.rs`, `crates/orb-shaders/src/wgsl/bezier.wgsl` | [`@mapbox/unitbezier`](https://github.com/mapbox/unitbezier) 1.0.0, from WebKit | BSD-2-Clause; [below](#unitbezier) |

| Polygon label anchors and their priority queue | `crates/orb-symbol/src/pole.rs` | GL JS's port of [polylabel](https://github.com/mapbox/polylabel), with [tinyqueue](https://github.com/mourner/tinyqueue) ordering | ISC; [below](#polylabel-and-tinyqueue) |

(Unicode-3.0) and [HarfRust](https://github.com/harfbuzz/harfrust) (MIT) as
Cargo dependencies; neither is vendored.

GL JS's render and query tests and their assets are not in the tree.
`cargo xtask fixtures` fetches them from the pinned GL JS commit into the
gitignored `fixtures/gl-js/` ([`fixtures/README.md`](fixtures/README.md)), so
they are not redistributed here, and they keep their upstream terms.
